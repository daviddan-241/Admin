Build a production-grade AI Digital Persona platform that creates a live autonomous avatar from uploaded videos, voice samples, and personality prompts.

CORE PURPOSE:
The platform should allow a user to upload videos/audio of themselves or consented media, train a digital persona, and then:

- appear as the AI avatar on webcam,
- transform voice in real time,
- auto-generate replies,
- act consistently with a configured personality,
- run while the user is offline.

MAIN FEATURES:

1. AI AVATAR TRAINING

- Upload 2–10 videos
- Extract facial identity embeddings
- Extract voice embeddings
- Learn speaking style and behavior
- Build reusable persona profiles
- Multi-persona support

2. REAL-TIME FACE TRANSFORMATION

- Webcam face replacement
- Expression transfer
- Eye tracking
- Lip-sync preservation
- Lighting adaptation
- Real-time rendering under 120ms latency
- OBS virtual camera support
- Browser webcam support

3. REAL-TIME VOICE TRANSFORMATION

4. PERSONALITY ENGINE
